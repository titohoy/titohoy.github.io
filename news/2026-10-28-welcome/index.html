The Eclipsed Realms website is open

A home for the game while it is still in development: the world, its people, and what the build can do today.

Eclipsed Realms is a dark medieval-fantasy, open-world cooperative action RPG in development for Windows PC on Unreal Engine.

This site is where we will keep the world, the story-so-far, the screenshots and the news. It will grow as the game does.

A few honest notes:

  • The game is in development. There is no release date, no price and no store page yet. When there is, it will appear here first.
  • Screenshots on this site are captured from development builds, with placeholder characters and test environments in places. We label them as such.
  • What we say about the story is spoiler-controlled. Pages marked deeper ask before they show you anything.

If you want to follow along, read the story, meet the Doll, or subscribe to the newsletter once it opens.
