The Bridge Troll

A hulking troll of the old bridges.

In development

Trolls were here before the roads. The Bridge Troll is one of the largest things you can meet on them, and one of the slowest to forget an insult.

It exists in the current build and is still being developed: where it waits and how it fights may change.
