The Plague Doctor

A beaked mask in the Witch Village, and the authority of someone who has seen a great deal of death.

In development

The beaked mask is the first thing you see and the last thing you forget. The Plague Doctor belongs to the Witch Village, a place apart from both the Cult and the Kingdom, and is among the most formidable people there.

The Witch Village is still in development, and so is the Plague Doctor’s part in it.
