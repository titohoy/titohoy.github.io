The Changeling

A lost boy, an invitation to dinner, and something at the table.

This entry discusses story events. It is kept behind a gate so you can choose.

A quest line in three chapters: A Child in the Woods, A Place at the Table, Behind the House.

It begins with a boy who has lost his way.

“I thought I knew the way. Then all the trees looked the same.”

It ends with a transformation that reveals a Changeling, and a dinner that did not go the way anyone hoped.

This entry is spoiler-gated because the story depends on not knowing what you are walking into.
