Creature

Mutant Bear

A bear that is a little too large, with its own way of moving.

It is still a bear, mostly. The proportions are wrong in a way you notice before you can name it, and it hits far harder than anything that size should.

What leaks through the broken Veil does not only change people. The Mutant Bear is one of the clearest signs that the damage has reached the animals.

Like its ordinary cousin, it can be tamed by someone patient and practised enough. Few people try twice.
