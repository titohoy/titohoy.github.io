Creature

The Cult Shaman

A senior keeper of the Cult's rites, at home in the Cult Village church.

Where the cultists follow the rites, the Shaman keeps them. You will find the Shaman in the church at the heart of the Cult Village, and a fight with the Shaman is a fight with one of the strongest people the Cult has.

The Shaman also trades in things the Cult values. Those who have survived the Arena’s ordeal are offered weapons made for demons, and the price is not paid in gold.
