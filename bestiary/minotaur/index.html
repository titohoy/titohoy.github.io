The Minotaur

A horned brute of the Arena. Wild things can be tamed; this one is no exception.

Horns, hooves, a body built for charging down anything that stands in the way. The Minotaur is among the Cult Arena’s famous hostile figures, Out in the world it is something stranger: a beast that the most patient handlers can tame.

Animal Taming, a perk in the skill tree, can bring even this creature to your side. It will follow you, fight for you, and be revived by you. It does not forget who raised its hand first.
