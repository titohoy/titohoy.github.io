Creature

Cultists

Robed, devoted and armed. The Cult's faithful, from frightened initiates to hardened ritualists.

Men and women of the Cult, in the village they have made their own, in the caves where they gather, and at the edges of every ritual. Some are new and uncertain. Others have stood around a ritual board many times.

They believe bloodshed earns favour, and they fight like people who believe it. They do not fully understand what they are calling on, which makes them dangerous to themselves as well as to you.
