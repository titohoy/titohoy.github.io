Creature

Wendigos

Antlered horrors of the deep Forest. Their cave is a place of Blood Relief.

Tall, antlered and wrong, the Wendigo is the wilderness made predatory. Two of them are met in the Forest, each in its own place and its own story.

One lurks near a house you will be invited to visit. The other nests in a cave marked with claw prints and dragged bodies, a cave associated with Blood Relief, tying the creature to the realm’s magical history.

They are boss-tier fights. They move fast, reach far and do not forget.
