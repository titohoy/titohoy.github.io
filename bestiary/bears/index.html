Creature

Bears

The Forest's oldest predator, and the reason some caves are best left alone.

Before anything else claimed the caves of the Forest, bears did. They are not monsters, and they are not corrupted. They are simply large, territorial and very good at what they do.

A bear does not care about your karma, your faction or your tradition. Walk into its den and it answers.

Bears can also be tamed, by someone who has learned enough about animals to try. A tamed bear follows you, fights beside you, grows stronger with you, and can be revived for a while if it falls.
