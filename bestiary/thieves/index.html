Creature

Thieves

Quick hands and quicker knives. They lay traps before they fight fair.

Not every Thief wants a fight, and the ones who do would rather it was short. Cross them and they drop bear traps where you are about to step, then come in while you are caught.

They can be dealt with, traded with or learned from. Being their enemy is a choice, and they remember it.
