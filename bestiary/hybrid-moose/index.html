Hybrid Moose

A moose that grew something it should not have.

In development

The silhouette is a moose until it turns. Then the extra growth is obvious, and so is the fact that it has noticed you.

The hybrids are the most visible evidence that supernatural damage has spread past the people who practised ritual. They gather where ordinary life was abandoned first.

The hybrids exist in the current build and are still being developed: how they fight and where exactly they roam may change.
