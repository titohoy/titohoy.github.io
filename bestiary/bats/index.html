Bats

A bat that bites for more than a bat should, and never settles.

They come out of the dark in a rush and do not land. A bat in Eclipsed Realms stays on the wing, circling, diving and biting, and it is harder to put down than its size suggests.

One is a nuisance. In a cave, with a torch going out, they are something else.
