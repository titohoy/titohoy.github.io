Who you become

Progression

You wake with nothing but a body and a temperament. What you grow into is earned: by fighting, by finding, by learning from people who should not have taught you.

Before you wake

Make the survivor

The character creator has three pages. Appearance: name, form, hair, eyes and clothing. Attributes: 12 points to spend at the start of a story, no more than 5 in any one. Personality: 15 points across Witty, Empathetic and Bold, the voice you will speak in.

Randomise any of it if you would rather be surprised. On a controller an on-screen keyboard takes your name. In co-op, every player builds their own survivor before joining, and everyone sees who you made.

Personality does not stop at the creator. The way you answer people keeps shaping it, and the game tells you when it shifts.

Experience

Levels and skill points

Experience comes from kills, from discovering the Cursed Doll’s waypoints (100 XP the first time each one is found) and from quests. Every level brings skill points.

In co-op, every player who hurt an enemy, directly or through their own summon, earns its full experience. The Doll’s training arena pays half. The PvP arena pays nothing; it is for pride.

Relief mastery

Unlock, enhance, master

Each tradition grows through its own perks. One perk unlocks spells one to four and then the Ultimate, rank by rank. A second enhances the spell of the same number. Mastery goes further.

Mastery changes what a spell is, not only how strong it is. A mastered Summon Demon stays bound to you. Mastered heals reach the allies standing in your aura. The magic.

Falling

Death is not the end, exactly

When you die you wake again at the nearest Cursed Doll, at full health, with your experience, attributes and perks intact. In co-op the others keep playing while you make your way back.

The road

Food and water

Hunger and thirst fall by five every minute. Keep them up and the road gives back.

Again, harder

New Game+ and New Game++

A finished story can be followed by a fresh one with more to spend: 18 attribute points in New Game+ and 25 in New Game++, against enemies with 1.2 and 1.4 times their health and damage. In co-op the session uses the host’s tier.

In development The tiers and enemy scaling work in the current build; unlocking them from the end of the story is still being finished.
