Nothing here is what it seems

Long ago, six blood anchors were bound into a prison for something that could not be destroyed. The prison broke. Kingdoms, creeds and cults grew over the wreck, and every one of them thinks it knows what lies beneath.

Relief magic

Write your spells

Throw down your Relief. Step into its aura. Then write the spell’s word, letter by letter, in front of everyone who is watching. Holy, Blood and Dark: three traditions, each with its own price.

Combat

Steel with weight

Lock on, read the swing, parry or roll. Sword, axe, greataxe, dagger, bow and torch each carry a signature technique and a ruinous ultimate. Elites turn faster at half health. Bosses punish habit.

Consequence

A world that remembers

Karma, corruption, faction reputation, relationships and personality are five separate records, and they are allowed to disagree. Witnesses talk. Guards arrest. People remember who you were when no one was looking.

Co-op

Never walk it alone

Take the campaign with up to three friends on your network, or share one screen in two-player split-screen. Every player keeps their own quests, karma and law record. A separate PvP arena hub holds up to eight.

Co-op details

Development build capture
The Arena

Settle it on the sand

Duel an NPC fighter or lead a squad in 2v2 to 4v4 team battles, or bring up to seven friends to a PvP hub. Everyone fights at level 100 with equipment drawn fresh every round, and nothing follows you back to the campaign.

The people

None of them are what they seem

All characters
Guide of the waypoint network

The Cursed Doll

She waits at the old waypoints, offers passage, instruction and warnings, and never quite says what she wants in return.

Spiritual leader of Start Village

The Priest

The spiritual authority of Start Village. His conviction is what makes him dangerous.

Elder of the Hidden Village

The Old Man

Keeper of the Holy tradition in the Hidden Village. He teaches what he knows, and weighs whether you should be taught it.

Court mage of the Kingdom

The Mage

The Old Man's son. A Blood Relief master who guards the King and Queen, and the one person the Hidden Village cannot reach.

Stage Jester of the Opera Hall

The Jester

Master of the Opera Hall stage. He turns your past into theatre, and reminds you that a second telling does not undo the first.
