Eclipsed Realms

A dark fantasy action RPG where the world keeps a record of you.

Eclipsed Realms is a dark fantasy action RPG in an open world. You explore, talk, fight in real time and cast written magic, alone or with up to three other players.

The game is in development. A release date, platforms, price and store pages have not been announced.

The world tour, the gameplay guide and the player handbook on this page need JavaScript. Turn it on and reload to see them.
