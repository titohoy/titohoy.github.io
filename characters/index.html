Those you will meet

Characters

A realm of believers, rulers, thieves and things that wait. None of them are simple, and few are safe.

Character

The Cursed Doll

She waits at the old waypoints, offers passage, instruction and warnings, and never quite says what she wants in return.

Character

The Priest

The spiritual authority of Start Village. His conviction is what makes him dangerous.

Character

The Nun

Once a healer of the plague-stricken. Now something that is no longer entirely alive or dead, and appears where the story is about to turn.

Character

The Old Man

Keeper of the Holy tradition in the Hidden Village. He teaches what he knows, and weighs whether you should be taught it.

Character

The Mage

The Old Man's son. A Blood Relief master who guards the King and Queen, and the one person the Hidden Village cannot reach.

Character

The Two Daughters

The Old Man's daughters. They keep the village's affairs, and they know where their brother is.

Character

The King and Queen

The rulers of the Kingdom. They cannot be treated as ordinary people, and the guards will not forgive you for trying.

Character

Mardo the Knife

A thief of the forest hub who teaches a dirty dagger trick to those who earn his notice.

Character

The Barmaid

She pours the drinks in Start Village's tavern, and she has seen worse than you.

Character

The Arena Attendant

The keeper of the arena hub, who sees that every fighter enters looking the part.
