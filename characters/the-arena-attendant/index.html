The Arena Attendant

The keeper of the arena hub, who sees that every fighter enters looking the part.

Every arena needs someone to run it. In the hub where players gather for duels and team battles, that is the Attendant: part host, part steward, entirely unbothered by bloodshed.

Speak to the Attendant to change how you look before you step onto the sand. What you wear in the arena stays in the arena.
