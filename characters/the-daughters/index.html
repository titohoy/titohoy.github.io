The Two Daughters

The Old Man's daughters. They keep the village's affairs, and they know where their brother is.

Two sisters, bound to a village that wants to be left alone and a brother who left it. They run errands, hold the village’s quarrels, and carry its quests.

They are also the ones who will ask a stranger to bring their brother home, a request that sends you across the realm to the Kingdom and into the presence of the Mage.

Neither is a mere messenger. They know the Old Man’s moods and the village’s patience, and they are the first to say what the village cannot.
