The Cult Arena

A ritual arena where captives face demons for an audience. Violence as liturgy and spectacle.

In development

The Cult does not hide its cruelty. It stages it.

The arena is where its victims are brought, one by one, to face what the Cult has called. Flesh Demons are sent to meet them in turn. Cultists watch.

“Those who enter are made to face them in turn.”

The arena has a presence in the story that goes beyond combat, and it is not a place you leave unchanged.

The Cult Arena is under active development; the area shown on this site is subject to change.
