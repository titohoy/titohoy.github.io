The Abandoned Village

A settlement emptied by something worse than plague. What lives there now came after.

In development

The houses are still standing. The tables are still laid. Nobody is coming back.

Whatever happened here happened quickly, and the animals that wander its lanes now are the clearest sign of what it was: hybrids of rat, moose and bird, corrupted past anything natural. Ordinary life gave way first in places like this.

The Abandoned Village is marked on the world map and is still being built.
