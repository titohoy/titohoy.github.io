Location

The Kingdom City

A walled mountain city of gates, guards, lowborn streets and noble halls.

Gates and drawbridges close behind you. Guards watch the streets. The upper city belongs to the crown, the lower to everyone else, and the dungeons to anyone caught in between.

The Kingdom’s laws are live in the open world. Commit a crime in sight and the response is immediate, an arrest, a bribe, a fight. The cells are real and the sentence is served.

The city also keeps theatre, and it keeps secrets. The crown is protected by a mage you will have to meet.
