Thieves' Hub

A forest camp of tents and banners where the Thieves trade in trust and take their time with strangers.

A clearing, a fire pit, cut logs, tents under a faded banner. The Thieves keep their hub in the open because no one who matters would dare to call it a hiding place.

A map marker names it. A stranger’s quiet approach does the rest of the introductions.

The hub is where the player meets the underworld of the Kingdom. Techniques taught here are cruel and useful, and the people who teach them are neither.
