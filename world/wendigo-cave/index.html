The Wendigo Cave

A cave in the deep Forest that was a bear's den first. It is not a bear's den now.

The claw marks at the mouth of the cave belong to a bear. The ones further in do not.

One of the Forest’s two Wendigos has made this cave its home, and the place carries an old association with Blood Relief: whatever the Wendigo is, it is tied to the same history as the magic you carry. Those who want that power have reasons to go in. Most of them have better reasons not to.
