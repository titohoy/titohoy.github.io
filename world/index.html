Living in the realm

What the road asks of you

Your own face

A three-page character creator decides who wakes in the ossuary. Nothing about your morality is decided for you.

Horses

Mount up and ride: carve turns, rear back, pivot on the spot, and hold to brake before you climb down.

Food and water

Hunger and thirst fall a little every minute. Eat and drink, or start paying for it.

Day, night and curfew

The realm keeps time. From ten at night to five in the morning the Kingdom enforces a curfew, and guards notice who is out.

Locks and pockets

A key opens a door. So does a steady hand and a well-timed pick. Pockets can be picked too, if the owner never sees you.

Falling and rising

Die, and you wake again at the nearest Cursed Doll.

New Game+ and New Game++

Finish a story and begin again with more attribute points to spend, against enemies that hit harder and last longer.
