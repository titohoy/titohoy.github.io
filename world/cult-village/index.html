Cult Village

The Cult's principal settlement: ritual, devotion, and the corruption that follows.

In development

A village that has made sacrifice its civic life. A church stands at its centre. Cultists tend it; the rest of the village is arranged around what they need.

Here the Cult’s rites are performed at scale, with more than the five participants of the lesser ritual, and with a result that is not meant for ordinary eyes.

Cult Village is in development. Its quests, characters and rituals will be revealed in future updates.
