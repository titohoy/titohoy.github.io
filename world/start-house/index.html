Location

The Old House

A house older than the village and the Cult. Beneath it, an ossuary. This is where you wake.

You wake among the dead, in a cage in an ossuary beneath an old house, and the first thing that speaks to you is the Doll.

The house is ancient. It predates the village above it and the Cult that haunts the village. Its stones belong to practitioners of the three Communions. It has an upstairs, an attic and a cellar, and the order in which you see them is part of the story.

“Beneath an old house. They put the things they wanted forgotten below it.”

Everything begins here, and not everything that happened here has finished happening.
