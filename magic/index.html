Holy · Blood · Dark

Magic

Magic here has a body. It is thrown down, written out and paid for.

How casting works

The Relief

1

Throw the Relief

A board-like object is thrown to the ground ahead of you. It grows and projects an aura around itself.

2

Stand in its aura

You can only cast from within the aura of your own tradition. Only one Relief of each tradition can lie in the world at a time.

3

Write the word

Casting is physical. The spell’s word is written across the board letter by letter, in the open, where anyone can watch.

4

Release

Cost and cooldown are paid only when the last letter lands. Interrupt the writing and nothing is spent.

The cost of the dark

Corruption is permanent

Dark spells need no stamina and no blood. They add Dark Points instead, and those do not wear off. At a hundred, the caster is corrupted for good: the Holy Relief refuses them, their health and stamina fall by a tenth, and their Dark magic comes faster and hits harder.

There is a way to turn corruption into power, and it is not offered to anyone else.

Beyond the school

Each ultimate has a rival

Every tradition has a default ultimate in slot five, and an alternate that can only be learned from someone in the world. The Old Man teaches one. The Kingdom’s court mage is associated with another. A fully corrupted player can learn a third.

The schools are not classes. A player can learn from all three and pay all three prices.
