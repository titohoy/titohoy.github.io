The Witch Village

A distinct supernatural settlement of gothic religious architecture and a cathedral. Its story is still being written.

In development

Beyond the Cult’s reach and apart from the Kingdom’s, the Witch Village is home to a second religious tradition, its own Nun, and a cathedral. It is its own place, with its own history.

This faction is in development. The settlement exists in the world, and its content is still to come. We will share more when it is ready.
