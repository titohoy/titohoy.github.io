Who holds the realm

Factions

The realm has no good side and no evil side. It has people with loyalties, fears and things to protect.

Faction

The Cult

An organised religious power that treats bloodshed as devotion. It does not fully understand what it is calling.

Faction

The Hidden Village

Druids and keepers of ancestral knowledge, far from the Kingdom's reach. Not automatic allies.

Faction

The Kingdom

The largest organised authority in the realm: a walled mountain city of order, law, guards, and its own secrets.

Faction

Guards, Knights and Templars

The Kingdom's armed hand: city guards, knights and templars, each a rung of the same ladder.

Faction

The Thieves

A criminal society with a camp of its own and a trade in knowledge that is not for sale to the Kingdom.

Faction

Start Village

A community of farmers, craftspeople and believers whose faith has begun to rot from the inside.

Faction

The Witch Village

A distinct supernatural settlement of gothic religious architecture and a cathedral. Its story is still being written.
