Description

Eclipsed Realms is a dark medieval-fantasy, open-world cooperative action RPG. Civilisation sits above the wreck of a failed supernatural prison. The player, an impossible survivor, learns three traditions of magic, thrown and written on a physical Relief, and chooses among factions that each hold a fragment of the truth.

Features

  • Three schools of Relief magic (Holy, Blood, Dark), with spells written letter by letter.
  • Lock-on melee, weapon techniques, shield parry, bow, stealth and taming.
  • Five separate ledgers of consequence: karma, corruption, faction reputation, relationships and personality.
  • Kingdom law with witnesses, guards, arrest, bribery and jail.
  • Up to four-player co-op; PvP arena hub.
